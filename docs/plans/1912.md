# #1912: holders claim tenant shares while the barrier is sealed

## Objective

Codex 7453 P1 15 (PR #1845, head 08f021085): the initialized Shamir branch of
`TenantBarrierView.svelte` renders the holder share-claim form only when the
barrier is unsealed. Tenant Shamir initialization leaves the barrier sealed,
so holders must be able to claim their shares before the first unseal quorum.
The claim form must render for every initialized Shamir barrier, sealed or not.
