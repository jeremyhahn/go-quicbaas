# Plan: merge requirements SF-MR-01..06 (#2364)

## Objective

Meet the merge requirements in docs/software-factory/requirements.md
(SF-MR-01..06). The merge preconditions are already on main:

- the review gate checks the PR is open, not a draft, green on its head and
  approved (`EvaluateReviewGate`);
- the merge is pinned to the reviewed head (`Hooks.Complete` refuses with
  `HeadMovedError` and passes the reviewed head to the forge);
- a settled conflict becomes `MergeConflictError`.

## Slices, in order

1. **SF-MR-06: after a merge, confirm the issues are closed** (this slice).
   `Hooks.confirmClosed` reads back every issue in `a.closes()` after a
   direct merge, a merge that already happened, or a train landing. It closes
   any the forge left open, with one marker comment
   (`quicbaas-sdlc:issue-closed pr=N`) naming the PR that landed the change
   and its merge commit. It is idempotent: closed issues are skipped and
   the comment is posted once.
   Tests (pkg/sdlc/hooks_test.go):
   - TestHooks_CompleteClosesAnIssueTheForgeLeftOpen
   - TestHooks_CompleteLeavesAClosedIssueAlone
   - TestHooks_CompleteClosesEveryClusterMember
   - TestHooks_CompleteConfirmClosedIsIdempotent
   - TestHooks_CompleteFailsWhenTheIssueCannotBeRead
   and pkg/sdlc/merge_train_delivery_test.go:
   - TestHooks_CompleteAfterTrainLandingConfirmsIssuesClosed
2. **SF-MR-04: queue order by re-verification cost** (next slice). The
   train queue orders changes by how much each would cost to re-verify, and
   not only by when they were enqueued.

## Out of scope

- SF-MR-05, the stranded-work detector, which #2366 covers.
