# Plan: SPEC row accounts (#1678)

## Objective

Ship SPEC row `accounts`: pooled provider accounts, the spend cap, cost
enforcement and job admission; the spend cap checked per turn, not only at
spawn; a worker session that rolls when its pooled account rotates. Folds
#785 and #1061 and the slice PR #2291. Detail: `docs/accounts/`.

## State on the row branch

- Pooled accounts, price book, billed and equivalent cost, per-account,
  project and tenant limits, spend deferral at admission (#1061): on main,
  recorded in `docs/plans/1061.md` (fold ref `row/1061-aq1061`, merged).
  Tests: `TestAccountResolverLeastUsageFailsOverPastASpentAccount`,
  `TestAccountResolverSpentPoolIsATypedDeferral`,
  `TestPlan_DefersAProjectOverItsOwnSpendLimit`.
- The spend cap per turn (#785, #782): PR #2291, merged into the row.
  `pkg/orchestration/turn_spend_gate.go` asks the cap before every turn of a
  pooled session and serialises a session's turns from the check to the
  usage record. Tests in `turn_spend_gate_test.go`:
  `TestPromptSession_RefusesTheNextTurnOfASessionOverItsAccountsWindow`,
  `TestPromptSession_RefusesATurnQueuedBehindTheTurnThatSpentTheAccount`,
  `TestPromptSession_RefusesATurnWhoseAccountCannotBeRead`,
  `TestLockSessionTurn_*`.
- A worker session rolls when its account rotates: on main.
  `session_account_freshness.go` answers whether a session's credential is
  older than its account's (generation, then rotation time); the SDLC runner
  replaces a stale session before its next turn (`pkg/sdlc/runner.go`,
  `replaceStaleCredential`). A limited turn rotates the session
  (`orchestrator_account_rotation.go`). Tests:
  `TestSessionAccountFreshness_*`, `TestJobRun_AccountRotatedIsSilentWhenNothingCanAnswer`,
  `TestRunner_AnAccountRotation*`, `TestRotateSessionAccount_*`,
  `TestDispatchTurn_LimitedTurnRotatesTheSession`.
