# Plan: SPEC row `cluster` (#1649)

## Objective

go-qrdb's cluster, membership and rebalance services reach QuicBaaS
consumers through `sdk/go`; QuicBaaS's own parallel cluster service and
client go on the owner's word; no bind, advertise or upstream address is
hardcoded (folded #185). The row's one PR marks the row shipped in
`docs/SPEC.md` and closes #1649.

## Slices, in order

1. **Fold earlier work** (done). Merge PRs #2270, #2432, #2343 and #2427
   with merge commits; account for both `refs/fold/cluster/*` refs
   (1801h merged, content-neutral; the sqi wip dropped, superseded by
   #2427's c5306e87d). Tests: the folded PRs' own (barrier port
   allocation, sockets-root fixture, OIDC signing store, sealed-window
   health, MCP KV, cluster builder), plus the unit tests of every
   package they touch.
2. **Re-export** in `sdk/go` (new `qrdb_cluster.go`):
   - cluster: `QRDBClusterClient` (`qrdbsdk.ClusterClient`),
     `QRDBClusterMember`, `QRDBMembershipEvent`, `QRDBAPIProtocol`;
   - membership: `QRDBRaftMember`, `QRDBMembershipManager`,
     `QRDBMembershipConfig`, `QRDBMembershipOption`,
     `QRDBMembershipStore`, `QRDBMembershipReconfigurer`,
     `QRDBNodeAdvertiser`, `QRDBAdvertiseConfig`, `QRDBAdvertiseOption`
     (go-qrdb `pkg/cluster`), and a `QRDBMembershipClient` interface
     that `*qrdbsdk.Client` satisfies;
   - rebalance: the existing `QRDBRebalanceClient` plus
     `QRDBShardStatsResponse`, `QRDBRebalancePlanRequest`,
     `QRDBRebalancePlan`, `QRDBRebalanceResult`, `QRDBImbalanceReport`.
   - `Client` gains `QRDBCluster()`, `QRDBMembership()` and
     `QRDBRebalance()` over the lazily built qrdb sub-client; the
     `cmd/quicbaas` test mocks gain the three methods.
   Tests: `sdk/go/qrdb_cluster_reexport_test.go`. Alias identity; over
   the embedded transport `QRDBCluster().AddReplica/Members/Leader` reach
   the stub `AdminServicer`; `QRDBRebalance()` on a transport without
   rebalance returns a typed error, not nil; `TransportEmbedded` without
   `WithEmbeddedQRDB` fails fast on all three.
3. **No hardcoded addresses** (#185): Raft projection literals,
   agent/inference upstream fallbacks, the dead per-protocol bind
   fields. Each address comes from config; tests show that a missing
   value fails instead of falling back to a literal.
4. **Owner's word**: remove `sdk/go/client/cluster`, the
   `quicbaas.Cluster` service and its handlers once the owner rules.
   Until then this slice is held, and the PR names it.
5. **Ship**: tick the row's items and mark the row shipped in
   `docs/SPEC.md`; update `docs/cluster/api.md` to the re-exported surface.

## Out of scope

- Embedded wiring of go-qrdb's rebalance, lease, watch and MVCC
  servicers into `QRDBEmbeddedServicers` (it would alias go-qrdb
  `pkg/service`, which SPEC keeps out of go-quicbaas): membership
  managers are built over network transports, or on the node itself.
- The go-qrdb/go-xkms pin bump (#1389).
