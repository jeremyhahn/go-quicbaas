# Plan: SPEC row `oci-registry` (#1684, folds #1348)

## Objective

The OCI distribution registry at `/v2/`, moved from go-trusted-platform's
`pkg/registry` to `pkg/ociregistry`. It has the referrers API. Blobs and
metadata go through the object-storage service (go-objstore). It
authenticates by mTLS workload identity and by TPM attestation through
go-xkms peergate, with attestation required by default. Design:
`docs/service-plane/architecture.md` § OCI registry.

Source: go-trusted-platform `pkg/registry` at
`34ba92bbaf42e924acae36618e99c5b539b8d0a2` (28 files, its tests included).

## Slices, in order

1. **Move the core.** Copy `pkg/registry` into `pkg/ociregistry`: the
   registry, storage, config, auth handler, upload limits, manifest
   handling, signing and encryption, with their tests. Imports from
   go-trusted-platform are replaced with go-quicbaas or standard-library
   code. The gRPC adapter waits for slice 4 because it needs
   go-trusted-platform's protos. Its tests, and `TestReferenceHelpers964`
   (which tests the adapter's reference helpers), wait with it.
   go-trusted-platform's `pkg/crypto/aesgcm` moves with the core, with its
   tests, to `pkg/ociregistry/internal/aesgcm`. Tests: the moved unit tests
   pass unchanged in `pkg/ociregistry`. (Done.)
2. **Storage through the object-storage service.** `QuicBaaSStorageClient`
   is backed by the go-quicbaas object-storage service for both blobs and
   metadata. Tests: a push and pull round trip against the in-memory
   object-storage backend, plus tenant isolation.
3. **Layer encryption with go-qrdb DEKs.** Remove `LocalKMSClient`. Tests:
   encrypt and decrypt with a DEK, and a wrong-tenant DEK is refused.
4. **Serve `/v2/` and the referrers API.** Mount the distribution handler
   on the node's REST server at `/v2/`. Add
   `GET /v2/<name>/referrers/<digest>`, filtered by `artifactType`. Expose
   it as `Compute().Images()` in `sdk/go`, served on all six transports.
   Tests: OCI conformance cases for push, pull and referrers, and
   `make integration-test-ociregistry`.
5. **Authentication.** mTLS workload identity goes through the go-qrdb
   auth chain. TPM attestation goes through go-xkms
   `pkg/attestation/peergate`, re-exported in `sdk/go/xkms`.
   `RequireAttestation` is on by default. This needs the go-xkms pin at
   v0.50.0-alpha or later; the pin is held under #1389. Tests: no
   certificate gets 401, an unattested peer is refused by default, and an
   attested peer is admitted.
6. **Close the row.** Mark it shipped in `docs/SPEC.md` and tick #1348.

## Out of scope (seams in other repositories)

- go-trusted-platform removes its `pkg/registry` and publishes registry
  names to DNS (its own row).
- go-xkms supplies peergate. It already exists in go-xkms v0.50.0-alpha
  and later.
