Branch `row/1659-audit-logging`, PR #2449. Detail: `docs/audit-logging/`.

## Objective

QuicBaaS audit logging runs through go-qrdb: the audit service
(`AuditClient`) is a re-export of go-qrdb's and the sink is go-qrdb's;
QuicBaaS's parallel audit model and store goes on the owner's word;
signature verification fails closed when signing is on; the HMAC signing
key is held in protected memory. Folded items: #23, #827, #1751, #1752.
