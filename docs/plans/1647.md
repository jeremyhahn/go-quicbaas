# Plan: SPEC row `codec` (#1647)

## Objective

The SPEC row `codec` holds as docs/SPEC.md states it:

1. go-qrdb's codec; no API surface of its own.
2. QuicBaaS's own codec package (`pkg/codec`, no importers) is a parallel
   implementation, removed on the owner's word.

Detail lives in `docs/codec/`.

## Findings

- go-qrdb carries its own `pkg/codec`; the embedded node encodes with it.
  go-qrdb's `sdk/go` re-exports no codec, and go-quicbaas needs none.
- `pkg/codec` has no importer outside its own tests.
- go-quicbaas still carries a codec surface that configures or serves nothing:
  - `config.CodecConfig` and `Config.Codec` (the `codec:` block): no reader.
  - `sdk/go` `CodecConfig`, an alias of the above.
  - `pkg/api/rest` `HandleGetOptimized` (no route; it calls `HandleGet`) and
    the helpers `detectPreferredCodec` and `setContentType` (no caller).

## Slices

1. Item 1 (this slice): remove the dead codec surface above and add guard
   tests that fail while it exists:
   - `pkg/config`: no `CodecConfig` type and no `Codec` field on `Config`.
   - `sdk/go`: no exported `Codec*` identifier, no `client/codec` package.
   - `pkg/api/rest`: no codec handler or codec helper.
   - root module: no non-test file outside `pkg/codec` imports `pkg/codec`
     (it stays parallel until the owner rules).
   `docs/codec/` says the codec is go-qrdb's and quicbaas has no codec
   config, handler or SDK surface.
2. Item 2 (blocked on the owner's word): delete `pkg/codec` and its
   Makefile build-configuration block, drop the no-importer guard, then
   mark the row shipped in docs/SPEC.md (sdlc.md §2b) in the row's PR.

## Out of scope

- The messaging channel codec (`MessagingCodec*`, `Channel.Codec`): that is
  a messaging field, owned by the messaging row.
- `pkg/storage/file`'s direct use of `go-codec`: storage row.
