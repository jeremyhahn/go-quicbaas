## Slices, in order

1. **Delete fence (#1303).** `sdlc.Store` gains a per-project delete fence
   kept in the same tenant KV as the bindings. Both sides write their own mark
   and then read the other's, so at least one side sees the other and a delete
   and a bind can never both succeed.
   - `FenceProjectDelete` admits one delete at a time: while a live fence that
     is not a tombstone is held, a second delete is refused with
     `sdlc.DeleteInProgressError` (a version conflict, 409). Only an absent or
     stale fence (older than `sdlc.DeleteFenceTTL`, a crashed delete) is
     replaced.
   - `DeleteProject` fences, checks for a binding, then deletes. On success it
     turns its own fence into a tombstone (`MarkProjectDeleted`, token-checked,
     on a detached 30s context); on a refusal or a failed delete it clears its
     own fence. A tombstone never goes stale and is never cleared: project IDs
     are uuids and never reused.
   - `PutBinding` stamps a fresh `WriteToken` on every write and returns the
     record it replaced, decided in the same CAS loop.
   - `SDLCService.Bind` reads the fence after every write, rebinds included.
     If the fence is held, or the read fails, it withdraws its own write
     (`WithdrawBinding`, by token; a rebind restores the prior record) on a
     context detached from the request, forgets the route only for a fresh
     bind, and returns `sdlc.ProjectDeletingError` or the read error. A failed
     withdrawal is logged and returned as `sdlc.BindWithdrawError`.
   Tests:
   - `pkg/sdlc`: fence round trip, staleness, a second delete refused while
     live, a stale fence replaced, the tombstone holding forever, the mark's
     token check; PutBinding's prior and token; withdrawal of a fresh write,
     of a rebind (prior restored), a token mismatch left alone.
   - `pkg/service`: the hooked race driven through `SDLCService.Bind` (refused,
     no binding, no route); a bind after a completed delete refused by the
     tombstone; a second delete refused; a refused delete clears its fence; a
     rebind under a fence restores the prior; a request canceled after
     `PutBinding` still withdraws.
2. **Docs and ship.** Remove the known gap from `docs/projects/architecture.md`,
   document the fence and the error in `docs/projects/`, tick the row's items,
   and mark the row shipped in `docs/SPEC.md`.
