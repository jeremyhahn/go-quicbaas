## Slices, in order

1. **Delete fence (#1303).** `sdlc.Store` gains a per-project delete fence in
   the tenant KV; each side writes its mark, then reads the other's.
   - `FenceProjectDelete` admits one delete at a time (`DeleteInProgressError`,
     409); only an absent or stale (`DeleteFenceTTL`) fence is replaced.
   - `DeleteProject` fences, checks for a binding, deletes, and tombstones its
     fence (`MarkProjectDeleted`, token-checked).
   - `PutBinding` stamps a `WriteToken`; `Bind` reads the fence after every
     write and withdraws its own write by token (`ProjectDeletingError`).
   Tests: fence round trip, staleness, tombstone, token checks, withdrawal;
   the hooked bind/delete race through `SDLCService.Bind`.
2. **Settlement fixes (review of slice 1), one commit each:**
   1. Per-project bind lease (`AcquireBindLease`/`ReleaseBindLease`,
      `BindLeaseTTL`, `BindInProgressError` as 409). Test: store_bind_lease_test.
   2. `Bind` and `Unbind` hold the lease from `retirePriorDelivery` to the last
      write. Test: of two interleaved binds the second is refused, writes none.
   3. `Bind` pre-checks the fence; a withdrawal restoring a prior binding
      re-onboards it (`BindWithdrawError`). Tests: rebind behind a fence; a
      fence after the pre-check re-onboards the prior.
   4. A failed `store.Delete` re-reads the project: gone tombstones, present
      clears the fence, unreadable leaves it held. Tests for each outcome.
   5. Round 2: a bind with no live fence withdraws if the project is gone; an
      unwritable tombstone returns `ProjectDeleteUnsettledError`; gRPC maps
      `BindInProgressError` to FailedPrecondition. Tests: late bind; held lease.
   6. Round 3: `Store.SettleBindingWrite` settles a write of unknown outcome
      (token landed, else a void marker or the re-stamped record) so a pending
      CAS never applies; `Bind` settles before the fence read. Tests hold the
      write back past bind and delete: no binding or route is left.
3. **Docs and ship.** Remove the known gap from `docs/projects/architecture.md`,
