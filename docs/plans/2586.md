# Plan: CI on GitHub-hosted standard runners (#2586)

## Objective

Every CI job runs on the standard GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-latest` runner. A job
is self-hosted only when it needs specific hardware or measurably exceeds a
standard hosted runner's disk, memory or 6-hour job limit, and then carries
its measured reason as `# self-hosted: <reason>` beside its `runs-on`.
Larger hosted runners are not used.

## Slices

1. Hosted-only runners (one commit):
   - `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: `unit` drops its shard split onto the
     bigdisk pool and runs on `ubuntu-latest`; `publish-images` drops its
     Gitea-only `release` arm. The `unit` steps guarded
     `runner.environment == 'self-hosted'` (pool checkout, setup-go, short
     TMPDIR, native `make coverage-shard`, TMPDIR cleanup) can no longer run
     and are removed. The header's runner comments state the hosted-only rule.
   - `.github/workflows/code-review.yml`: `scope` and `gate` drop their
     Gitea-only `bigdisk` arm. `review` (Codex runner) and `review-opencode`
     (Ollama model host, never run on github.com) stay as they are.
   - `test/buildgates/ci_runners_test.go`: `TestCIRunsHostedFirst` holds the
     rule: every ci.yml job's `runs-on` is `ubuntu-latest` unless its line
     carries `# self-hosted: <reason>` (none does), and no `runs-on` is an
     expression that can pick a self-hosted label.
     Mutation cases kept or added: a job sent to a self-hosted label, the
     split expression put back, a waiver comment with an empty reason.
     Dropped: the split's shard-list cases and the pool-step cases
     (GOPROXY, GOFLAGS, integrity script, WAN downloads, short TMPDIR).

## Tests

- `go test ./test/buildgates/` (TestCIRunsHostedFirst,
  TestRunnerProblems_Mutations, TestRunnerProblems_Unparsable).

## Out of scope

- Other workflows (all already on `ubuntu-latest`), the Makefile, scripts/
  (`scripts/ci-go-integrity.sh` stays; it passes on a hosted runner).
- Any other change to code-review.yml.
