# Plan: #2657, a 16g IN_IMAGE_MEMORY cap for Lint and the devcontainer image job

## Objective

On self-hosted runners golangci-lint is killed at the 8g sidecar cap
(#2200). The Lint job and the devcontainer image job get a 16g
`IN_IMAGE_MEMORY` cap there; a GitHub-hosted runner keeps no cap.

## Slices

1. `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the two jobs' `IN_IMAGE_MEMORY` expression
   gives `16g` on self-hosted runners, `none` on `ubuntu-latest`.
   Test: `test/buildgates/ci_runners_test.go` asserts the cap per job
   (16g for Lint and the image job, 8g for every other capped job).
   Run with `make test-tooling`.
2. Merge `origin/main` (merge commit), keeping main's #2661 golangci-lint
   heap bound beside this cap.

## Out of scope

- The 8g cap of the lint-gates and integration jobs, and the sidecar cap
  of #2200 on other jobs.
- The integration suite's own failures (`make integration-test` needs
  Docker; the job does not gate yet, row #1700).
