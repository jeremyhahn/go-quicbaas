# Plan: refuse to publish a commit that carries an AI attribution trailer (#2361)

## Objective

The publisher already refuses to push an unsigned commit, or one signed by an
untrusted key. Nothing refuses a commit whose message credits an AI tool, so
such a commit is published. The publisher must refuse it before it pushes,
whether or not the project signs its commits.

## Slices

1. **Publisher trailer check** (this slice).
   - `pkg/sdlc/publish.go`: `VerifyTrailers` reads the outgoing range
     (`origin/<base>..refs/heads/<branch>`, the ref being pushed) with
     `--format=%H%x1f%B%x1e` and tests every message line against the
     attribution rule (a Co-Authored-By naming an AI vendor, or a
     "Generated with/by" footer naming one). `Push` calls it after
     `VerifySigned` and before `admit`.
   - `pkg/sdlc/errors.go`: `ErrAttributionTrailer` and
     `AttributionTrailerError` (task, commit, offending line).
   - `pkg/sdlc/runner.go`: `refusedPush` parks the task escalated
     (FaultUntrustedSigner) with a reason that names the commit, the line,
     and the way out.
   - Tests: every vendor in the rule, mixed case, leading spaces and a
     "Generated with" footer are refused and push nothing; a human
     co-author, "generated by the build" and an empty message pass; the
     check reads the ref being pushed; it runs without signing; the runner
     parks escalated; the typed error names its subject and unwraps.

## Out of scope

- The merge train's own handling of refused pushes
  (`merge_train_build.go`): unchanged in this slice.
- Rewriting or repairing a refused commit: a person replaces it and
  readmits the task.
