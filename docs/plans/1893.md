# #1893: the literal system tenant for device-certificate issuance

## Objective

Codex review 7420 P1 4 on #1845: `CertificateService.IssueDeviceIdentity`
read its tenant through `prepareTenantContext`, which maps the system binding
to `""`. The CA's EK/AK/IDevID issuers stamp the request's tenant into the
certificate and refuse none, so a valid system-tenant device identity failed
with an internal error.

The #1845 cut (ruling 198) dropped the adapter line that forwards the tenant
(`TenantID: req.TenantID` in `caServiceAdapter.IssueDeviceIdentityCert`), so
this row re-lands it together with the fix.

## Slices

1. Test first, `pkg/server/ca_service_adapter_extensions_test.go`: a
   system-tenant EK and AK identity driven from the service facade
   (`service.NewCertificateService`) through the production adapter onto a
   real go-xkms CA, recording the request the CA's TCG issuers receive. The
   certificate must be signed by the CA and the CA asked to issue in the
   literal system tenant. Fails on main (no tenant reaches the CA). The
   pinned go-xkms (v0.49.1) does not yet stamp or require the tenant on
   TCG credentials; #1845's bump does, which is where the finding bites.
2. Fix:
   - `pkg/server/ca_service_adapter_extensions.go`: the adapter forwards
     `req.TenantID` on the `TrustedCACertificateRequest`.
   - `pkg/service/certificate_extensions.go`: the facade names the tenant
     `tenantctx.Target` resolved (the literal system tenant, the only one
     allowed to issue device identities) instead of `prepareTenantContext`'s
     `""`.

## Out of scope

- Ordinary issuance (`IssueCertificate`, `SignCSR`) and its tenant mapping.
- Tenant-scoped device identities (go-xkms #1764).
