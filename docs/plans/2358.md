# Plan: ready-queue order and hygiene (#2358)

## Objective

The factory orders ready issues by blast radius and priority:* labels only.
Issues a release waits on, and issues in a milestone, should rank ahead, and
open issues the queue cannot rank or pick up should be reported.

## Slices

1. Ranking and pure hygiene functions (this slice).
   - `PriorityInputs.ComputedForIssue` in `pkg/sdlc/priority.go`: a
     release-blocker or release-candidate label adds 1600 (above
     priority:critical at the ceiling); a milestone adds 100 (orders issues
     inside a tier, never across tiers). The adopter, the issue source and
     the parked-issue source rank through it.
   - `QueueHygiene` and `HoldLowPriority` in `pkg/sdlc/triage_queue.go`:
     pure functions over listed issues, no forge calls.
   - Tests: `TestComputedForIssue_*` in `priority_test.go`;
     `TestQueueHygiene_*` and `TestHoldLowPriority_*` in
     `triage_queue_test.go`.
2. Wire `QueueHygiene` into the poll so each cycle reports the gaps, and
   `HoldLowPriority` into issue selection, with the stuck-PR count and
   milestone state supplied by the poller. Tests through the issue source
   fakes.

## Out of scope

- Milestone due dates: `ForgeIssue` carries the milestone title only.
- Writing labels or milestones on issues; the factory only reports gaps.
