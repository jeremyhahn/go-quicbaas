# Plan: #1888 — tenant Shamir setup expects plaintext shares (Codex 7395 P1 3)

## Objective

The tenant barrier Shamir init response is share-free: go-qrdb's
`TenantBarrierInitResponse` (pin v0.30.1-alpha) carries `threshold`,
`total_shares` and `share_count`, never `shares`, and REST returns it
unchanged (200 for Shamir, 204 otherwise). `TenantBarrierView.svelte`
still assigns `resp.shares` (undefined) to `revealedShares`, so the
`!== null` guard renders `ShamirSharesReveal`, whose `shares.length`
throws and breaks the view after a successful Shamir init.

## Slices

1. Failing test first: `cmd/quicbaas/frontend/test/TenantBarrierView.test.ts`
   drives a Shamir init against the real share-free REST body
   (`{tenant_id, strategy, threshold, total_shares, share_count}`) and
   asserts the view renders a "shares sealed to officers" summary
   (threshold, total, sealed count, claim pointer) and never the
   plaintext-shares reveal. Fails before the fix.
2. Fix:
   - `sdk/typescript/src/types.ts` (+ tracked `dist/types.d.ts`):
     `TenantBarrierInitResponse` drops `shares`, gains `share_count`.
   - `TenantBarrierView.svelte`: hold the init summary (threshold, total,
     share_count) instead of plaintext shares; render a sealed-to-officers
     notice with a dismiss that reloads status; the structural `init`
     adapter returns no shares.

## Out of scope (seam)

- Holder certificates in the response and the inline officer roster in
  the tenant init request: the go-qrdb pin carries neither
  (`TenantBarrierInitRequest` has no officer field, the response no
  holders). That part is a ruling 159 seam on go-qrdb (rulings 172/183),
  reported to the coordinator, not built here.
