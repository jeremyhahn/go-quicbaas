# Plan: SPEC row `barrier` (#1650)

## Objective

Deliver the `barrier` row of `docs/SPEC.md`: go-quicbaas carries go-qrdb's
system barrier as a re-export, served on all six transports, with no seal
service of its own; barrier secrets never sit in plain strings or forced argv;
the never-served share verbs are gone. The row's one PR (`row/1650-barrier`)
closes #1650 and marks the row shipped.

## Slices, in order

1. **Re-export complete** (acceptance 1). Test: `sdk/go`
   `TestQRDBBarrierClient_EveryVerbReexported` walks go-qrdb's method set and
   fails on a verb or signature type with no sdk/go alias. Done.
2. **Fold refs** (owner order 11-13): `row/226-aq226` merged (the PIN half of
   slice 7); the #1465 and #1822 plans folded into slices 8 and 9; aq237's
   go.sum residue dropped. Done.
3. **No parallel seal service** (acceptance 2, 3, #1559). `pkg/service`
   `BarrierService` drops its own result types for go-qrdb's. Test: the
   `pkg/server` parity test covers each verb on all six transports.
4. **init-shamir removed** (#1830, ruling 142 (a)): proto, REST, gRPC, MCP,
   CLI, Go and TS SDK. Test: each transport answers the verb as unknown.
5. **Never-served share verbs removed** (acceptance 6, #1497) from
   `barrier.proto`, the sealed-window allowlist and `sdk/typescript`. Test:
   the allowlist and TS SDK tests.
6. **Protected memory** (acceptance 4, #237): shares, recovery keys and PINs
   in wipeable buffers in the CLI and service. Test: wipe after use.
7. **Off argv** (acceptance 5, #226): PINs (merged in slice 2,
   `cmd/quicbaas/pin_input.go`) and the passphrase take a file or env path.
   Test: `pin_input_test.go`; integration on every transport.
8. **MCP enable-encryption contract** (#1465, m579 item 3 (B)). One contract,
   go-qrdb's (`tenant_id`, `strategy`, `mechanism`, `availability`,
   `passphrase`, `shamir_threshold`, `shamir_total`) on standalone and
   composed: the SDK stops sending `target_strategy`, standalone serves
   go-qrdb's tool, and `executeBarrier{,Tenant}EnableEncryption` go as a move.
   Test: SDK MCP transport on both deployments, system and tenant scope, and
   a tenant admin refused on another tenant. **Blocked on the go-qrdb pin
   (#1389)**: v0.30.1-alpha's tool lacks tenant-scope authz (m586,
   go-qrdb#797 in v0.51.0-alpha).
9. **Quorum proofs bind the purpose** (#1822, m1074 (3) option 1). go-xkms
   factors check go-qrdb's `ChallengeMessage(challenge_id, purpose, nonce)`
   (go-xkms#1684, v0.62.0-alpha). Test: a `pkg/server` composed test where a
   bare-nonce proof is refused and a purpose-bound one accepted, system and
   tenant; `BarrierView.svelte` shows the message to sign, with a unit test.
   **Blocked on the stack re-pin (#1389)**, itself waiting on go-libsignal.
10. **Ship**: `docs/barrier/` updated, the row marked shipped in `docs/SPEC.md`.
