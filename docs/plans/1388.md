# Plan: #1388 iam: the tenant in the X.509 subject OU

## Objective

Owner ruling (rulings.md f518455): the X.509 subject `OU` carries the tenant
on every layer. go-qrdb#777: the OU and the SPIFFE SAN are both carried and
must agree. Ruling 62(a): follow go-xkms's OU form and identify an agent by
its SPIFFE kind, not by an `OU=agents` stamp.

## Slices (in order)

1. **One OU rule in `pkg/iam`.** `TenantFromSubjectOU` reads the tenant
   from the first subject OU; `CheckCertOUTenant` refuses a certificate
   whose OU names a tenant other than the one its SPIFFE SAN names
   (`*CertOUTenantMismatchError`). `TenantFromPeerCert` (REST login, PIV
   login, OAuth2 mTLS) applies it.
   Tests: OU agrees -> tenant; OU disagrees -> `TenantUnresolvedError`
   wrapping the mismatch; no OU -> SAN tenant (legacy certificate).
2. **Every other transport applies it.** gRPC tenant interceptor and the
   apimodule certificate login check the OU against the tenant
   `TenantFromClientCert` returns. Tests: a mismatching certificate is
   refused on each.
3. **Fleet issuance stamps OU=tenant.** `FleetNodeCertificateSubject`'s OU
   defaults to the tenant; `DefaultFleetNodeCertOU` (`agents`) is removed,
   and the embedded node and MCP preset resolver stop overriding it.
   Tests: issuer tests (fake, SDK and real CA) see OU=tenant.
4. **Fleet enrol and heartbeat recovery read the tenant from OU.** No
   `OU=agents` requirement; the tenant comes from OU and must match the
   request and the SAN; a SAN of kind `user` is refused (the agent is a
   `svc` workload). Tests: OU=tenant accepted, OU mismatch refused, a
   `user`-kind SAN refused, the old `OU=agents` certificate refused.
5. **Docs.** docs/agent-fleet and changelog.d: the certificate subject
   changed (callers re-issue fleet certificates).

## Out of scope

- The go-qrdb/go-xkms pin bump (#1389). On the pinned go-xkms v0.49
  `SignCSR` keeps the CSR subject, so go-quicbaas stamps the OU in its own
  CSRs; the generic issue/CSR-sign paths that take a caller's CSR follow
  with the bump, where go-xkms stamps the OU itself.
- A trust domain (and so a SAN) on the embedded fleet node's certificate:
  without a SAN its agent role is not checkable by kind; enrol keeps
  warning about such a certificate, as today.
- Authorization rows (#1372).
