# Plan: review and CI requirements (issue 2363)

## Objective

Close the gaps in SF-RV-01..07 and SF-CI-01..05
(docs/software-factory/requirements.md). Most are already met: cancelled
runs are void and reruns are budgeted (pkg/cifault, checks_triage.go), no
reviewer is dispatched on a red pipeline, a new head is reviewed again, and
review rounds are capped with escalation.

## Slices, in order

1. Self-approval and response deadlines (this slice).
   - `GateInput.AuthorLogin`, wired from the pull request's author at both
     gate call sites. An approval from that login (trimmed, case-insensitive)
     never counts. When the approvals fall short and one was the author's,
     the gate refuses with `GateBlockedSelfApproval` (SF-RV-07).
   - `EvaluateResponseDeadlines` (pkg/sdlc/response_deadline.go), a pure
     evaluator: a settled red check on the head is overdue after 1h
     (SF-CI-04). A request for changes on the head is overdue after 2h and
     a defect after 24h (SF-RV-03). A newer head is the response.
   - Tests: `TestEvaluateReviewGate_RefusesSelfApproval`,
     `TestEvaluateReviewGate_SelfApprovalDoesNotCountButOthersClear`,
     `TestEvaluateReviewGate_EmptyAuthorLoginChangesNothing`,
     `TestGateBlockReason_Pending` (new reason not pending), and
     `TestEvaluateResponseDeadlines` (table).
2. Later: act on `EvaluateResponseDeadlines` in the sweeps, telling the
   worker when a response is overdue and escalating a defect.

## Out of scope

- Any forge call or notification from the deadline evaluator.
- Changes to CI classification, rerun budgets, review dispatch or round
  limits.
