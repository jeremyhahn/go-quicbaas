### Security

- Self-service registration now creates the account in the broker, and the WebAuthn ceremony only binds a credential to it. Begin creates the tenant and a pending account with a derived handle, grants its roles, then opens the ceremony through the invite path. Finish binds the credential and activates the account. go-xkms `AllowPublicRegistration` is now always `false`, so a ceremony can no longer create an account with the email as its username. A failed or abandoned registration now removes its tenant and account instead of leaving an orphan tenant behind (#482).
- A self-registrant now holds `Admin` before the WebAuthn ceremony opens, so a software authenticator can no longer enrol a new tenant's administrator under tenant assurance `any`. An unfinished registration records its expiry on its tenant, in a record only the broker writes. A caller who may update a user therefore cannot mark a tenant for deletion. After a restart the server discards expired registrations and arms a timer for each unexpired one, so a restart mid-ceremony no longer leaves them behind. A tenant is discarded only while it holds nothing but the registrant's pending account (#482).
